Free templates

Logging is the highest return habit on this site and the one most people skip. These are the sheets to do it with. No email, no sign-up, no watermark.

Which one do I need?

If you train, take the workout log. If you are changing your weight, add the 12 week tracker. Take the food and protein diary only for the first two weeks, long enough to learn what your normal day adds up to. The cheat sheet is the whole site on one printable page.

How to actually use a log

  1. Write the set down as you finish it

    Not at the end of the session and not from memory. Thirty seconds of your rest period, which you are spending on your phone anyway.

  2. Record reps in reserve, not just weight and reps

    Three sets of 8 at 60 kg with 3 reps left is a different session from the same numbers with 0 left. Without that column you cannot tell whether a stall is effort or fatigue.

  3. Check last week before your first set

    This is the entire point. You are trying to beat a specific number, not to have a workout.

  4. Review every four weeks, not every day

    Daily numbers are noise. Four week trends are signal. The 12 week tracker is built around that rhythm.

Apps work too

Any free logging app is fine, and several are excellent. The format matters far less than the habit. These templates exist so that not owning the right app is never the reason you did not start.

Use them however you like

These templates are released into the public domain. Copy them, edit them, print them for your gym, hand them to clients, put your own logo on them. No attribution required and nothing to buy.
