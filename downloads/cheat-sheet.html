Nutrition foundation ~80%

  • Calories decide your weight. Set a deliberate target, not an accidental one.
  • Protein 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg bodyweight, every day.
  • Consistency 85% adherence for a year beats perfect for three weeks.
  • Sleep 7 to 9 hours. Steps 7,000 or more.

Training foundation ~75%

  • Show up 2 to 4 times a week, for years.
  • Effort 1 to 3 reps left in the tank on working sets.
  • Progression beat last week's logbook. Write it down.
  • Cover all six movement patterns each week.

Nutrition, tune next 15-20%

  • Fat floor 0.6 to 1.0 g/kg, carbs fill the rest. Ratio is preference.
  • Fibre 25 to 38 g a day.
  • Roughly 80% whole foods, for satiety and micronutrients.
  • Water to pale yellow urine. Budget alcohol as calories.

Training, tune next 15-20%

  • 10 to 20 hard sets per muscle per week. Start low.
  • Each muscle twice a week.
  • 1 to 2 exercises per muscle, kept for months.
  • Full range of motion, control the lowering.

Nutrition, later 1-2%

  • Creatine monohydrate 3 to 5 g daily. Works.
  • Caffeine 3 to 6 mg/kg pre-training. Works, watch sleep.
  • Meal timing, fasting windows, carb cycling: preference only.
  • Fat burners, BCAAs, detoxes: skip.

Training, later 1-5%

  • Rest 2 to 3 min compounds, 1 to 2 min isolations.
  • Hardest lift first, isolations last.
  • Easy week when several lifts stall for 2 to 3 weeks.
  • Drop sets and supersets save time, not better for growth.
